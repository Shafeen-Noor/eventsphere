EventSphere · LTV

Lifetime value — pay-per-event model

EventSphere charges per event, not monthly SaaS. LTV = gross margin from all events a host pays for over their relationship, minus acquisition cost. Assumes paid-only (no free tier) · Essential $19 · Premium $39.

Repeat-event assumptions

Hosts buy once per event. Repeat comes from birthdays, anniversaries, clients, and venue partners.
