Talk track

Small profit. Fair for a birthday.

AWS costs me about one to three dollars to host a full Pro event. I charge nine to thirty-nine. After payment fees I keep a small operating margin — enough to run the product, not a luxury markup.

  • Competitors charge $25–$120 for one night.
  • I undercut the mid-shelf for small parties and still cover cloud cost.
  • $29/mo subscription is for planners who run events every month.
