Pricing recommendation

Move off $9 — protect margin on every channel

At $9, Essential cannot survive a realistic paid CAC. Raising Essential to $19 and Premium to $39 keeps Free as the land, still undercuts “venue photo apps,” and makes both channels profitable.

Plan Who it’s for Old Recommended Why
Free Trial · 5 guests · 40 uploads · 24h $0 $0 Same. Acquisition engine for both channels.
Essential Birthday / dinner · 50 guests · 500 uploads · 7 days $9 $19 Still impulse-cheap; now profitable after ads CAC.
Premium Wedding / big party · 200 guests · unlimited · 30 days $29 $39 Anchors partner pitches; strong contribution.

Pitch line: “Start free. Upgrade once for the event — $19 or $39 — not another monthly subscription.”

Assumption register

Numbers you can walk through in the room

Paid ads funnel

  • Budget: $1,000 / month
  • CPM $25 → 40,000 impressions
  • CTR 2.5% → 1,000 clicks ($1 CPC)
  • Click → free create: 12% → 120 free events
  • Free → paywall: 60% → 72
  • Paywall → buy: 76% → 55 paid
  • Click → buy: 5.5%
  • CAC: $18 ($1,000 ÷ 55)
  • Target mix: 40% Essential / 60% Premium

Partnership funnel

  • Active partners: 30
  • Events touched / partner / month: 5
  • Pitch rate: 60% → 3 pitches
  • With free: pitch → free 80% → paid 83% → 2.0 / partner
  • No free: pitch → pay 67% → 2.0 / partner
  • Network: 60 paid events / month (both scenarios)
  • Mix: 35% Essential / 65% Premium
  • Commission: 20% of price, after sale only
  • Blended CAC: ~$6.40 (same — % of revenue)

No free tier · ads funnel

  • Same budget: $1,000 → 1,000 clicks
  • Click → start checkout: 9% (was 12% free create)
  • Checkout → paid: 50% (was 76% paywall → buy)
  • Click → buy: 4.5% → 45 paid (was 55)
  • CAC: $22 (was $18)
  • Essential after CAC: −$5.47 · Premium: +$11.85
  • Month profit @ 60% Premium: +$221 (was +$502)

Replace with interview-backed rates when available. Prefer conservative partner counts over optimistic ad CTRs. If Essential must stay $9 for product reasons, keep ads Premium-only in creative — do not claim Essential survives a $15–18 CAC.
